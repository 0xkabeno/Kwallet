warp-plus v1.2.6 - https://github.com/bepass-org/warp-plus
  The prebuilt Android arm64 executable from the project's GitHub release (warp-plus_android-arm64.zip,
  SHA-256 585f6f8579d8f5df0c26c874fc1730c4f4298085dbed815ad2d559c8a07c6af5; the executable inside it,
  warp-plus, SHA-256 0cac565d1818a26f0d81a4a0f408ccce116d9420d252b5783a31f198ff313043). Unmodified. CI downloads and
  checks it and packages it as hwallet's lib/arm64-v8a/libwarpplus.so; it is never committed to this repository.
  Hwallet runs it as a local SOCKS5 proxy for its own traffic only ("VPN for Hwallet", Settings > Network & VPN).
  License: MIT (text below). The executable is built by its authors from Go modules that include, among others,
  Psiphon tunnel core (github.com/Psiphon-Labs/psiphon-tunnel-core, GPL-3.0), wireguard-go based code and
  sagernet/gvisor / sing (see the project's go.mod). The GPL-3.0 parts are compatible with Hwallet's GPL-3.0-or-later;
  their sources are available from those projects. Cloudflare WARP and Psiphon are services of their operators;
  neither is affiliated with Hwallet.
